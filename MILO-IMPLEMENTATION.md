# Milo Implementation Contract

The embedded MiloAgent must retain its native scheduler, scanning, engagement, curation, action and safety behavior.

Creator Growth Suite changes to Milo are limited to:

1. Traffic/growth destinations:
   - YouTube
   - Rumble
   - Bandcamp
   - Amaze / Teespring

2. Daily target metrics:
   - 1,000 unique subscribers
   - 35,000 long-form views
   - 1,500,000 qualified Shorts views
   - 200 sales conversions
   - 1,000 unique visitors

3. Creator Growth action cadence: 3 minutes.

AgentTube independently handles YouTube/Rumble content optimization and creation.

Trend + Script and Video Use remain manual creator tools.

The daily targets are optimization targets, not guaranteed results.
